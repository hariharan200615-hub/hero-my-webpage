About HomeHero

A local team that treats your home like their own

HomeHero started in 2015 with a simple frustration: finding a reliable tradesperson took days of phone calls and guesswork. We built a network of vetted, well-paid professionals and put honest pricing in front of every booking.

Today we serve more than 30 neighbourhoods with a full-time quality team that reviews every job and follows up on every rating below five stars.
